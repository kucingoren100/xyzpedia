## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an eccentric, sculptural collage aesthetic defined as an inflatable sticker universe pressed onto pastel paper. Rooted in physical toy metaphors, neo-brutalist precision, and sticker-book tangibility, it completely rejects conventional corporate minimalism. Interface elements do not mimic software windows; they behave like punched-out vinyl stickers, inflated plastic ribbons, and physical paper panels.

### Brand Personality & Philosophy
- **Tactile Pop Brutalism:** Extreme typographic scale meets warm, pillowy geometry. Monolithic, squished display headlines form architectural landforms over which hyper-saturated stickers and matte 3D tubes tumble.
- **Dimensional Contrast:** Flat, unshaded paper grounds exist alongside pseudo-3D matte plastic forms, held together by precise, razor-sharp 1px ink outlines.
- **Physical Tangibility:** Cards and interactive targets avoid artificial drop shadows or diffused blurs. Every surface is an opaque cut-out laid deliberately on colored paper planes.

### Target Audience & Emotional Impact
Designed for creative-first decentralized finance, Web3 native communities, and modern creative platforms seeking irrepressible optimism, irreverence, and unforgettable tactile joy. It evokes curiosity, child-like playfulness, and decisive design confidence.

## Colors

The palette operates exclusively within a crisp **Light Mode** framework. The chromatic structure is segregated into architectural paper grounds, stark carbon contours, and a hyper-saturated 6-color sticker palette.

### Structural Base
- **Carbon (`#000000`):** The structural armature. Used for all primary typographic headers, tight 1px borders, high-impact CTA button fills, and marquee bands.
- **Paper White (`#FFFFFF`):** Primary canvas surface, interactive button interiors, card base surfaces, and inverted typography on dark fills.

### Atmospheric Paper Washes (Surfaces)
- **Sky Wash (`#DCEEFF`):** Level 1 ground. Applied as expansive, full-bleed hero backdrops.
- **Concrete Gray (`#CCCCCC`):** Level 3 ground. Applied to transition blocks and structured utility panels.
- **Soft Mist (`#E9E9E9`):** Interactive resting states, inactive pill fills, and disabled card backdrops.

### Sticker & Dimensional Accents
Colors are intentionally freed from legacy UI semantics:
- **Ember (`#FB4903`):** Primary fiery catalyst. Applied to explosive sticker icons, urgent badges, and dominant brand moments.
- **Voltage Violet (`#5C4ADE`):** Deep anchor accent. Used for secondary focus panels, download card structures, and featured sticker illustrations.
- **Electric Blue (`#4DA2FF`):** Dominant sculptural tone. Strictly reserved for 3D inflatable ribbons and structural section backgrounds. *Never use for body links or primary interactive fills.*
- **Mint Pop (`#55DB9C`):** Vibrant decorative fill for badges, highlight strips, and playful graphic stickers.
- **Lavender (`#E9CCFF`):** Soft pastel surface wash for accent cards, secondary tags, and badge planes.
- **Sunburst (`#FFD731`):** High-energy visual pop for graphic coins and badges. *Forbidden behind body typography.*

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the squeezed, eccentric personality of Bricolage Grotesque for monolithic display declarations with the balanced, modern grotesque cadence of Hanken Grotesk for interface structure and narrative prose.

### Display Architecture
- **Bricolage Grotesque:** Set with ultra-heavy weights (`800`) and radically tightened leading (`0.75` to `0.80`). Headlines function as massive sculptural architecture rather than mere text, interacting directly with layered sticker badges and 3D ribbons.
- **Hanken Grotesk:** Crisp and neutral with purposeful weight distribution (`500` for reading layers, `700` for interface controls).

### Structural Rules
- Always apply tabular figures (`tnum`) for numerical data inside tables and pricing modules.
- Navigation pills, button labels, and marquee strips are set in uppercase or title case using `label-pill` or `marquee` tokens with deliberate tracking (`0.03em` to `0.04em`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy is founded on an expansive, fluid grid anchored by bold section-to-section color blocks and intentional macro-whitespace. 

### Grid & Composition
- **Page Container:** Max-width constrained to `1440px` with fluid edge buffers.
- **Section Breaks:** Hard cuts across full-bleed pastel wash grounds (`#DCEEFF`, `#FFFFFF`, `#CCCCCC`). No gradient transitions or divider lines are allowed.
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Standard 4px baseline module scaling outward into expansive hero buffers (`space-hero` at `180px`).
- **Internal Card Padding:** Uniformly locked to `24px` (`space-lg`), producing dependable framing around dense interface components.

### Form Factor Behavior
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stack with `margin` of `1rem`. Hero typography scales to mobile display tokens. Component gap collapses to `space-sm` (`8px`) or `space-md` (`16px`).
- **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 12-column layout with 24px gutters. Asymmetrical card positioning simulates hand-placed paper collages.

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally eliminates traditional software drop shadows. Visual depth is engineered through an interplay of physical planar stratification and sculptural 3D objects.

### Depth Rules
- **No Drop Shadows:** `box-shadow: none` is an absolute system invariant across all light-mode surfaces.
- **Hand-Cut Ink Line:** Every interactive card, floating pill, and graphic badge is bounded by an unmistakable, uniform `1px solid #000000` perimeter.
- **Planar Z-Index Stacking:**
  1. *Substrate (Z: 0):* Full-bleed pastel paper grounds (`Sky Wash`, `Concrete Gray`, `Paper White`).
  2. *Atmosphere (Z: 10):* 3D inflatable matte tubular ribbons in Electric Blue winding across layout axes.
  3. *Typography Monument (Z: 20):* Monolithic display headlines anchored directly into the layout ground.
  4. *Tactile Foreground (Z: 30):* Floating paper cards, QR modules, and die-cut sticker badges.
  5. *Fixed Overlay (Z: 50):* Sticky navigation pill cluster and dynamic carbon marquee band.

## Shapes

The design system embraces an exaggerated, hyper-rounded visual identity (Roundedness level 3). Shapes mimic rounded die-cut vinyl stickers, stamped coin medallions, and pill capsules.

### Shape Hierarchy
- **Pill Shape (`rounded-full` / `9999px`):** Used universally for all primary CTAs, ghost buttons, navigation containers, status indicators, and marquee chips.
- **Card Radius (`rounded-2xl` / `20px` to `24px`):** Applied to content cards, modal windows, and structured graphic sticker frames.
- **Elevated / Shell Radius (`rounded-3xl` / `32px` to `40px`):** Used on overarching canvas wrappers and large feature panels.
- **Uniform Border Rule:** All radiused components retain the mandatory `1px solid #000000` outline, preserving a crisp, graphic comic-strip edge.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Filled Primary CTA:**
  - Background: Carbon (`#000000`)
  - Typography: Paper White (`#FFFFFF`), `label-pill`, uppercase
  - Border: `1px solid #000000`
  - Radius: Pill (`rounded-full`)
  - Padding: `10px 16px`
  - Hover: Subtle scale transform (`scale(1.02)`) with no shadow change.
- **Ghost / Nav Pill:**
  - Background: Paper White (`#FFFFFF`) or Soft Mist (`#E9E9E9`)
  - Typography: Carbon (`#000000`), `label-pill`
  - Border: `1px solid #000000`
  - Radius: Pill (`rounded-full`)
  - Padding: `12px 20px`

### Cards & Surfaces
- **Standard Content Card:**
  - Background: Paper White (`#FFFFFF`)
  - Border: `1px solid #000000`
  - Radius: `20px` (`rounded-2xl`)
  - Padding: `24px` (`space-lg`)
- **Accent Feature Card:**
  - Background: Lavender (`#E9CCFF`) or Voltage Violet (`#5C4ADE` with white text)
  - Border: `1px solid #000000`
  - Radius: `24px`
  - Padding: `24px`

### Die-Cut Stickers & Badges
- Graphic badge elements mimicking physical stickers are enclosed in `1px solid #000000` outlines with `16px` to `20px` corner radii.
- Stickers can be arbitrarily rotated between `-3deg` and `+4deg` to disrupt strict digital grids and reinforce hand-pasted realism.
- Color fills: Ember (`#FB4903`), Mint Pop (`#55DB9C`), Sunburst (`#FFD731`), or Voltage Violet (`#5C4ADE`).

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- Background: Paper White (`#FFFFFF`)
- Border: `1px solid #000000`
- Radius: Pill (`rounded-full`) or soft card corner (`16px`)
- Typography: `body-md` in Carbon (`#000000`)
- Focus State: Border thickens to `2px solid #000000`; no glow rings or drop shadows.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Radio controls are fully circular (`50%`); checkboxes use a distinct `6px` radius.
- Both feature a crisp `1px solid #000000` outline.
- Active Fill: Primary Ember (`#FB4903`) with a stark white mark.

### Marquee Strip
- Continuous horizontal ticker running flush to section borders.
- Background: Carbon (`#000000`)
- Typography: Paper White (`#FFFFFF`), `marquee` token, uppercase with `0.04em` tracking.
- Height: Fixed compact band (`32px` to `36px`), perfectly framing scrolling announcements.
